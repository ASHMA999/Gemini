## Program Overview

This comprehensive training program is designed to equip professionals with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills in Artificial Intelligence. The program follows a hands-on methodology where every unit is paired with real tools that trainees can use immediately in their work environment.

## Learning Philosophy

- Every concept is immediately followed by a hands-on exercise with a real AI tool
- Industrial scenarios from Arab Potash Company are used throughout the program
- Data security and privacy are woven into every unit, not treated as an afterthought
- Trainees build a personal Prompt Library they can use immediately at work
- The program culminates in a real-world capstone project from the trainee's own department

---

## Target Audience

- IT leaders and CISOs evaluating AI adoption strategies
- Engineers (Production, Maintenance, Safety) seeking AI-powered efficiency
- HR professionals looking to leverage AI for recruitment and training
- Data analysts and business intelligence professionals
- Any knowledge worker who interacts with documents, data, or communications daily
