## Section C: Practical Application (12-13 points each | 25 points total)

**9. Maintenance Scenario Prompt (13 points)**

**Scenario:** You are a maintenance planner at a petrochemical facility. Centrifugal pump P-101A (Sulzer CPT 23-1, installed 2019) on the cooling water circuit has been showing elevated vibration readings (4.2 mm/s vs. baseline of 1.8 mm/s) over the past two weeks. You need to generate a preliminary troubleshooting guide for your maintenance technicians.

Write a complete, production-ready prompt that addresses this scenario. Your prompt must include: **(Scoring below)**
- A clear role and action statement **(3 points)**
- Specific equipment and operational context **(3 points)**
- Defined output format and structure **(3 points)**
- Safety and regulatory constraints **(2 points)**
- Audience specification **(2 points)**

Write a complete prompt that HR could use to generate this job description. Your prompt must include: **(4 points each)**
- Context about the company and role requirements
- Specific sections the job description must contain (e.g., responsibilities, qualifications, reporting structure)
- Constraints on tone, length, and compliance (e.g., equal opportunity language, industry-appropriate terminology)
