## Detailed Plan Per Component

### 1. Program-Wide Materials

#### A. Program Schedule (`program-schedule.md`)
- Day-by-day, hour-by-hour breakdown of all 36+ hours
- Session timing with breaks, prayers, and meal times
- Materials checklist per day
- Room setup requirements (projector, Wi-Fi, power strips)

#### B. Facilitator Master Guide (`facilitator-master-guide.md`)
- Training methodology: 70% hands-on, 20% discussion, 10% lecture
- Facilitation tips for each activity type
- How to handle common questions and pushback
- Troubleshooting guide (Wi-Fi down, tool access issues, etc.)
- Energy management: when to use energizers, when to take breaks
- Arabic/English bilingual delivery tips

#### C. Pre-Training Assessment (`pre-training-assessment.md`)
- 20-question survey: current AI knowledge, tool experience, expectations
- Self-assessment matrix: rate yourself 1-5 on each unit topic
- Department and role identification for group formation

#### D. Post-Training Assessment (`post-training-assessment.md`)
- Mirror of pre-assessment for before/after comparison
- Confidence rating on each skill area
- Action plan: "3 things I will implement in my first week back"
- Net Promoter Score question

#### E. Participant Welcome Kit (`participant-welcome-kit.md`)
- Welcome letter from program director
- Pre-requisite checklist: accounts to create before Day 1
  - Google account (for AI Studio, NotebookLM)
  - OpenAI account (for tokenizer, ChatGPT)
  - Claude.ai account
  - Meshy.ai account
- Laptop requirements and software to install
- What to bring: work documents/data for exercises (anonymized)

#### F. Prompt Library (`prompt-library.md`)
- Cumulative collection of all prompts taught across 8 units
- Organized by: department, task type, framework used
- Copy-paste ready templates
- "Fill in the blanks" versions for customization

#### G. Evaluation Forms (`evaluation-forms.md`)
- Daily session feedback (3 questions, quick)
- End-of-program comprehensive evaluation
- Trainer self-evaluation checklist

### 2. Presentations (Per Unit)

Each presentation follows this standard structure:

| Section | Duration | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Opening Hook | 3-5 min | Surprising statistic, video, or question |
| Learning Objectives | 2 min | "By the end of this unit, you will be able to..." |
| Core Content | 60% of time | Key concepts with visuals, examples, demos |
| Live Demo | 15% of time | Trainer demonstrates tools in real-time |
| Hands-On Exercise | 20% of time | Participants practice with guided instructions |
| Recap & Key Takeaways | 5 min | 3-5 bullet summary, preview of next unit |

Presentation format: Markdown slides (compatible with Marp, Slidev, or reveal.js for conversion to PowerPoint/PDF)

---

### 3. Worksheets (Per Unit)

Each worksheet includes:
- **Objective Statement**: What the trainee will practice
- **Step-by-Step Instructions**: Numbered, clear, screenshot-ready
- **Workspace**: Blank areas for writing prompts, recording observations
- **Reflection Questions**: "What surprised you?" "How would you use this at work?"
- **Key Takeaway Box**: Summary of the most important lesson
- **Challenge Extension**: Optional advanced task for fast finishers

### 4. Activities Design

#### A. Ice Breakers (8 activities, one per unit)

| Unit | Ice Breaker | Duration | Description |
|------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | "AI or Human?" | 10 min | Show 10 outputs - guess if AI or human wrote them |
| 2 | "Prompt Roulette" | 10 min | Random word generator - build a prompt from 3 random words |
| 3 | "Microsoft Bingo" | 8 min | Bingo card with M365 features - find someone who uses each |
| 4 | "Department Swap" | 10 min | Describe your biggest work challenge to someone from another dept |
| 5 | "Spot the AI Art" | 8 min | Gallery of images - which are AI-generated vs real photos? |
| 6 | "Research Race" | 10 min | Who can find a specific fact fastest: Google vs AI vs NotebookLM |
| 7 | "Data Detective" | 8 min | Show a chart with a hidden anomaly - first to spot it wins |
| 8 | "Pitch Your AI" | 10 min | 30-second elevator pitch of your capstone idea |

#### B. Energizers (12 activities, use between sections)

| # | Energizer | Duration | Type |
|---|-----------|----------|------|
| 1 | "Token Auction" | 5 min | Bid tokens to buy AI capabilities - learn token economics |
| 2 | "Temperature Dance" | 3 min | Stand up: move left (low temp) or right (high temp) based on scenario |
| 3 | "Hallucination Court" | 5 min | One person presents AI output, others play jury: real or hallucinated? |
| 4 | "Prompt Telephone" | 5 min | Whisper game but with prompts - see how meaning degrades |
| 5 | "AI Speed Dating" | 5 min | 1-minute paired conversations: share one AI tip you learned today |
| 6 | "Framework Flash Cards" | 4 min | Hold up a scenario card - teams race to shout the right framework |
| 7 | "Copilot Charades" | 5 min | Act out an M365 Copilot feature, others guess |
| 8 | "The Constraint Game" | 4 min | Add increasingly ridiculous constraints to a prompt - when does it break? |
| 9 | "Reverse Engineering Race" | 5 min | Given an AI output, reverse-engineer the prompt that made it |
| 10 | "AI Myth Busters" | 4 min | True/False rapid fire about AI facts |
| 11 | "Data Story in 30 Seconds" | 3 min | Look at a chart for 10 seconds, then tell its story from memory |
| 12 | "The Robot Walk" | 3 min | Physical: walk like a robot, then like a human - discuss AI limitations |

#### C. Understanding Check Activities (Per Unit)

| Unit | Activity | Type | Duration |
|------|----------|------|----------|
| 1 | "Concept Map Builder" | Individual | 10 min - Draw connections between tokens, context, temperature, hallucinations |
| 2 | "Framework Match Game" | Pairs | 8 min - Match scenarios to frameworks, compare with partner |
| 3 | "Copilot Feature Scavenger Hunt" | Teams | 12 min - Find and demonstrate 5 features you didn't know existed |
| 4 | "Prompt Before & After Gallery" | Group | 10 min - Post weak and strong prompts on wall, gallery walk |
| 5 | "Precision vs Beauty Debate" | Teams | 10 min - Debate: when is AI art enough vs when must it be engineering? |
| 6 | "Tool Selection Tournament" | Bracket | 10 min - Given scenarios, pick the right tool - elimination rounds |
| 7 | "Insight Showdown" | Teams | 12 min - Same dataset, different teams - who finds the best insight? |
| 8 | "Peer Review Circle" | Group | 15 min - Present capstone, receive structured feedback cards |

#### D. Group Challenges (Cross-Unit Team Competitions)

| Challenge | Spans | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| "The AI Department" | Units 1-4 | Teams represent departments. Score points for best prompts across all exercises |
| "Build-a-Bot" | Units 3, 6 | Design a complete Copilot Agent + NotebookLM knowledge base for your department |
| "The Data Olympics" | Unit 7 | 3 rounds of data analysis challenges, judged on speed + insight quality |
| "Capstone Showcase" | Unit 8 | Shark Tank-style presentations. Audience votes + trainer scores |

### 5. Applications (Hands-On Exercises)

Each unit's application file contains:
- **Guided Exercises**: Step-by-step with expected outcomes (from the original curriculum)
- **Independent Practice**: Similar tasks without step-by-step guidance
- **Real-World Challenge**: Use your own work data/documents
- **Extension Tasks**: Advanced challenges for fast learners
- **Troubleshooting Tips**: Common errors and how to fix them

Total exercises across all units: **30 guided + 16 independent + 8 real-world = 54 exercises**

---

### 6. Assessments

#### Per-Unit Quizzes (Units 1-7)
- 10 questions each: 5 multiple choice, 3 short answer, 2 practical
- Aligned to learning objectives
- Include answer key with explanations

#### Capstone Rubric (Unit 8)
- 5 criteria (from original curriculum): Prompt Quality (30%), Tool Mastery (25%), Practical Value (25%), Security Awareness (10%), Presentation (10%)
- Detailed descriptors for each score level (1-5)
- Peer review scorecard
- Self-assessment reflection form
