# The Challenge: Regulatory Inspection Preparation

> **Scenario:** APC has been notified of an upcoming regulatory inspection by the Jordanian Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (MEMR). Your department has **3 weeks** to prepare. You must use AI tools to accelerate the preparation process.

### The Inspection Will Cover:
- Equipment maintenance records and compliance
- Safety incident history and corrective actions
- Environmental monitoring data and reporting
- Spare parts inventory and critical spares availability
- Staff training and competency records
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) currency

**Your mission:** Use the AI skills from Units 1-7 to prepare a comprehensive inspection readiness package.

---

# Project Requirements

You must deliver **all six components** using AI tools learned in this program:

| # | Deliverable | Primary AI Tools |
|---|------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Compliance Gap Analysis** | NotebookLM + Deep Research |
| 2 | **Equipment Readiness Report** | Data analysis (ChatGPT/Gemini) |
| 3 | **Safety Incident Summary** | Text mining + heatmaps |
| 4 | **SOP Currency Review** | NotebookLM + document analysis |
| 5 | **Critical Spares Audit** | Inventory analysis + forecasting |
| 6 | **Presentation Package** | AI-assisted document generation |

> Each deliverable must include the AI prompts used, the raw AI output, and your verified/edited final version.

---

# Deliverable Details (1-3)

### 1. Compliance Gap Analysis
- Upload relevant regulations to NotebookLM
- Use Deep Research to identify recent regulatory changes
- Map current APC practices against requirements
- Identify and prioritize gaps

### 2. Equipment Readiness Report
- Analyze maintenance data for overdue PMs and open work orders
- Generate equipment availability and reliability metrics
- Create KPI dashboard for inspection presentation
- Flag any equipment operating beyond recommended service intervals

### 3. Safety Incident Summary
- Analyze 2-year incident history with AI text mining
- Generate heatmaps (location x time, type x shift)
- Document corrective actions and their completion status
- Highlight trends and improvement initiatives

---

# Deliverable Details (4-6)

### 4. SOP Currency Review
- Upload SOPs to NotebookLM
- Check revision dates and identify outdated procedures
- Cross-reference SOPs against current regulations
- Prioritize SOPs needing immediate update

### 5. Critical Spares Audit
- Perform dead stock analysis on warehouse inventory
- Verify critical spares availability for key equipment
- Run demand forecast for high-priority items
- Identify any critical spares with zero stock or long lead times

### 6. Presentation Package
- Executive summary (1 page) for plant management
- Detailed findings report (5-10 pages) for inspection team
- Visual dashboard of key metrics
- Action item tracker with owners and deadlines

# Prompt Quality Rubric (30%)

| Score | Level | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **25-30** | Excellent | Prompts are specific, include context and constraints, demonstrate iterative refinement, use advanced techniques (chain-of-thought, role assignment) |
| **18-24** | Good | Prompts are clear and specific, include relevant context, show some iteration |
| **10-17** | Adequate | Prompts are functional but generic, limited context, minimal refinement |
| **0-9** | Needs Work | Prompts are vague, missing context, no evidence of iteration |

### What We Look For:
- Did you provide APC-specific context in your prompts?
- Did you iterate when the first response was insufficient?
- Did you use system prompts or role-setting effectively?
- Did you break complex tasks into manageable steps?
