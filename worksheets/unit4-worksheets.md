## APC Prompt Formula — Reference Card

Cut out or bookmark this section for use during all exercises.

### The APC Formula

```
A — Actor:    WHO should the AI act as? (role, expertise, years of experience)
P — Purpose:  WHAT should the AI produce? (specific deliverable, goal, action)
C — Context:  Background details (industry, constraints, audience, format, data)
```

### APC in Action — Example

| Element | Bad Version | Good Version |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **A** (Actor) | "You are an AI" | "You are a senior industrial engineer with 15 years of experience in petrochemical plant operations" |
| **P** (Purpose) | "Write a report" | "Write a root-cause analysis report for the Q3 compressor failure, identifying the top 3 contributing factors and recommending corrective actions" |
| **C** (Context) | (none) | "The audience is the plant maintenance committee. Use formal technical language. Include a timeline table. Limit to 2 pages." |

### APC Quick Checklist

Before sending any workplace prompt, verify:

- [ ] Did I define a specific **Actor** with relevant expertise?
- [ ] Is the **Purpose** clear, specific, and measurable?
- [ ] Did I provide sufficient **Context** (audience, format, constraints, data)?
- [ ] Would a human expert receiving these instructions know exactly what to do?
