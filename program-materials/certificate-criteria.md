# Certificate Criteria

## Applied AI Training Program - Certification Framework

---

## Certificate Levels

### Level 1: Certificate of Completion

**Requirements:**
- Attended at least 80% of total program sessions (4 out of 5 days minimum)
- Completed Pre-Training Assessment
- Completed Post-Training Assessment
- Participated in at least 5 hands-on exercises across the program

### Level 2: Certificate of Distinction

**Requirements (all of the above PLUS):**
- Submitted a Capstone Project
- Capstone score >= 4.0 / 5.0
- Completed all unit worksheets
- Active participation in group challenges

### Level 3: Certificate of Excellence

**Requirements (all of the above PLUS):**
- Capstone score >= 4.5 / 5.0
- Recognized by peers (top 3 in peer review voting)
- Demonstrated exceptional prompt engineering skills
- Created at least 3 original prompts added to the class Prompt Library

---

## Scoring Breakdown

### Capstone Project Scoring (100 points total)

| Criterion | Weight | Points | Scoring Guide |
|----------|--------|--------|---------------|
| Prompt Quality | 30% | 30 pts | 6 pts per level (1-5): Framework used correctly, constraints clear, iterative improvement shown |
| Tool Mastery | 25% | 25 pts | 5 pts per level: Used 3+ tools, appropriate tool selection, demonstrated features |
| Practical Value | 25% | 25 pts | 5 pts per level: Solves real problem, immediately usable, measurable impact |
| Security Awareness | 10% | 10 pts | 2 pts per level: Data privacy considered, enterprise-safe practices |
| Presentation | 10% | 10 pts | 2 pts per level: Clear, concise, showed process not just results |

### Score Conversion

| Points | Rating | Certificate Level |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| 90-100 | 5.0 - Excellence | Level 3 eligible |
| 80-89 | 4.0-4.4 - Distinction | Level 2 |
| 60-79 | 3.0-3.9 - Competent | Level 1 |
| Below 60 | Below 3.0 | Completion only (if attendance met) |
