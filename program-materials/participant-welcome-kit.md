## What to Expect

### Daily Schedule

- **Start:** 8:00 AM
- **End:** 4:30 PM (varies by day)
- **Breaks:** Morning break, Prayer + Lunch break, afternoon break
- **Format:** Short lectures + live demos + lots of hands-on practice

This is NOT a sit-and-listen program. You will:

- Use AI tools from the very first hour
- Work individually AND in teams
- Compete in friendly challenges
- Build a personal Prompt Library you'll take home
- Create a real project for your department (capstone)

1. **Prompt Library:** A personal collection of tested, optimized prompts for your role
2. **Tool Confidence:** Know which AI tool to use for which task
3. **Security Awareness:** Understand what's safe to share with AI and what isn't
4. **Capstone Project:** A real solution for a real problem in your department
5. **Certificate:** Program completion certificate from APC AI Excellence Program

## Program Ground Rules

1. **Experiment fearlessly** - There are no wrong prompts, only learning opportunities
2. **Share generously** - Your best discovery might be your neighbor's breakthrough
3. **Ask questions anytime** - If you're confused, someone else is too
4. **Respect data privacy** - Never paste real confidential data into free AI tools (we'll explain why in Unit 1)
5. **Stay present** - Close non-essential tabs and put phones on silent during exercises

## Quick AI Terminology Glossary

| Term | Simple Explanation |
|------|-------------------|
| **Token** | The smallest piece of text AI understands (roughly 0.75 words) |
| **Prompt** | The instruction you give to AI |
| **Context Window** | How much text AI can "remember" at once |
| **Temperature** | Controls how creative vs. precise the AI is |
| **Hallucination** | When AI confidently makes up false information |
| **LLM** | Large Language Model - the AI engine (like GPT, Claude, Gemini) |
| **RAG** | A secure way for companies to use AI with their own data |
| **Fine-tuning** | Customizing an AI model for specific tasks |
| **Few-shot** | Teaching AI by giving it examples |
| **Chain-of-Thought** | Asking AI to think step by step for better answers |
