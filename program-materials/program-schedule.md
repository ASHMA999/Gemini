## Room Setup Requirements

### Per Day

- Projector with HDMI/USB-C connection
- Reliable Wi-Fi (minimum 50 Mbps for 20+ concurrent users)
- Power strips at every table (1 per 2 participants)
- Whiteboard or flip chart + markers
- Printed worksheets (per participant, per unit)
- Name tags and table numbers for group assignments

### Participant Requirements

- Laptop with Chrome or Edge browser
- Pre-created accounts (see Welcome Kit)
- Notebook and pen for reflections
- Work documents (anonymized) for real-world exercises
